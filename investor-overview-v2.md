# BabyBitcoin — Investor Overview

## One-Liner

**We replace $100k lawsuits with $10 AI arbitration.**

---

## What We're Building

BabyBitcoin lets two parties enter a contract where **AI resolves disputes instead of lawyers**.

Both sides agree upfront. If a dispute arises, AI evaluates whether terms were met and releases funds automatically.

No courts. No lawyers. No delays.

**See it live:** [demo.babybitcoin.xyz]

---

## The Problem

Smart contracts are great at math, but terrible at judgment.

They can't determine:
- whether work was "good enough"
- whether terms were reasonably followed
- whether qualitative standards were met

**The cost is massive:**
- Median contract dispute costs small businesses **$91,000**
- **12 million lawsuits** filed against small businesses every year
- Resolution takes **months to years**

Most real-world contracts still rely on slow, expensive, and subjective human arbitration.

---

## The Market

The freelance economy is exploding — and so are disputes.

- **76 million freelancers** in the US (36% of workforce)
- **$1.27 trillion** contributed to US economy annually
- **51% of freelancers** have experienced wage theft (not paid for completed work)
- **20% of gig workers** cite payment disputes as a major issue

These are people who can't afford lawyers. They need a better way.

---

## The Solution

BabyBitcoin adds **AI judgment** to smart contracts.

Two mutually consenting parties:
1. Enter a contract on-chain
2. Agree to an AI as the neutral third party
3. Let the AI resolve disputes if they arise

The contract settles automatically based on the AI's decision.

**Speed:** Disputes resolved in seconds, not months
**Cost:** $10 vs. $91,000
**Trust:** Transparent, on-chain, no conflicts of interest

---

## Why Trust AI?

- **Consent-based:** Both parties agree to AI arbitration upfront
- **Transparent:** Decision process recorded on-chain for anyone to verify
- **No conflicts:** AI has no financial stake in the outcome (unlike lawyers billing hours)
