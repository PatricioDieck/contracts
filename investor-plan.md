# **BabyBitcoin — Investor Overview (External)**	

## **What We’re Building**

BabyBitcoin lets two parties enter a contract where **AI resolves disputes instead of lawyers**.

If both sides agree in advance, an AI evaluates whether contract terms were met and releases funds automatically.  
 No courts. No lawyers. No delays.

---

## **The Problem**	

Smart contracts are great at math, but terrible at judgment.

They can’t determine:

* whether work was “good enough”

* whether terms were reasonably followed

* whether qualitative standards were met

As a result, most real-world contracts still rely on slow, expensive, and subjective human arbitration.

---

## **The Solution**

BabyBitcoin adds **AI judgment** to smart contracts.

Two mutually consenting parties:

1. Enter a contract on-chain

2. Agree to an AI as the neutral third party

3. Let the AI resolve disputes if they arise

The contract then settles automatically based on the AI’s decision.

This makes arbitration:

* dramatically faster

* significantly cheaper

* harder to corrupt

---

## **Initial Use Case**

We are starting with **one narrow category of contracts** where:

* disputes are frequent

* outcomes are subjective

* traditional arbitration is slow and expensive

Examples include:

* digital service agreements

* IP usage and licensing

* escrow-style performance contracts

We are intentionally focused on shipping **one working utility**, not a broad platform.

---

## **Why Now**

This was not possible until recently.

* AI can now evaluate qualitative outcomes

* Blockchain can now enforce decisions trustlessly

Together, they unlock a new class of contracts that could not exist before.

---

## **Token Utility (High-Level)**

The BBTC token is used to:

* pay for AI arbitration

Demand is driven by **utility**, not speculation.

---

## **Long-Term Opportunity**

The current focus is execution, not vision.

# **BabyBitcoin — Internal Build Plan (Not Investor-Facing)**

This section is **execution-only**.  
 Investors should **not** see this.

---

## **Build Philosophy**

* Speed over perfection

* Utility before network

* Do not couple traction to fundraising

* Ship one thing that works, then expand

The goal is not to “launch a blockchain.”  
 The goal is to **get AI arbitration live and used** as fast as possible.

---

## **Phase 1: Ship Utility Fast**

### **What We Build First**

* One AI-arbitrated contract type

* Minimal UI

* Clear success / failure criteria

* Real users and real money (even small)

Guardrails are acceptable:

* semi-on-chain logic

* off-chain AI inference

* human override if needed

Speed matters more than decentralization.

---

## **Where to Build First (Fastest Path)**

**Ethereum-compatible infrastructure only.**

Preferred options:

* **Base** (fast adoption, strong ecosystem)

* **Arbitrum** (mature tooling)

* **Optimism** (enterprise-friendly)

No custom L1.  
 No new consensus.  
 No infra for infra’s sake.

Goal: ship fast using existing wallets, dev tools, and liquidity.

---

## **Phase 2: Token Goes Live After Utility**

Order matters:

1. Utility is live

2. Token is introduced

3. Token is used for arbitration \+ inference

4. Usage creates demand

Avoid:

* token-first launches

* speculative narratives

* promised utility

---

## **Phase 3: Functional Network Expansion**

Only after:

* contracts are used

* disputes are resolved

* value is flowing

Then:

* expand contract types

* add multiple AI agents

* introduce decentralized verification

* harden infra

This is where network effects start.

---

SaaS exists to:

**The network is the asset. SaaS is the interface.**
